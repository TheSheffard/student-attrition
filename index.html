Simulation methodology

How the system works

This deployment runs entirely in the browser. Uploaded academic records are evaluated with a deterministic attrition-risk simulation; no request is made to FastAPI or any other backend.

The simulator weighs academic indicators such as CGPA, attendance, course completion, failed units, carryovers, repeated courses, GPA trend, and probation history. It then presents a simulated probability, a binary result, and Low/Medium/High tiers.

Your login session and saved prediction runs are kept in this browser's localStorage. They remain available after a refresh on the same browser and device, but they are not synced to other devices.
